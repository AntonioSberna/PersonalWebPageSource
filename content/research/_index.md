## What I am working on lately

My starting question was a practical one: how do you make an existing building safe while spending as little as possible? Over the years that question has widened in three directions.

**From cost to environmental impact.** Money is not the only price of strengthening a building: materials and construction also have an environmental footprint. My recent work looks for solutions that [balance both](/proceedings/genetic-algorithm-framework-for-environmentally-sustainable-and-cost-efficient-seismic-retrofitting-design-of-reinforced-concrete-structures/).

**From one building to a whole region.** Detailed computer models are too slow to study every building in an area. I am interested in simpler models that keep what matters, so that seismic risk can be assessed at the scale of a region.

**From computer tools to AI agents.** I have long used algorithms to search for good designs. Now AI agents can carry out entire parts of a research or engineering project, and I want to understand what this means for how reliable engineering decisions are, and who is accountable for them. I wrote some first thoughts [after ECEE 2026](/blog/2026-ecee-science-w-agents/).
